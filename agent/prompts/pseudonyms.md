Some values are pseudonyms, replaced by code before you see them and put back when you call a tool or submit: host-N and host-N.lan are internal hosts, value-N internal hosts or other internal names; site-N.lan are internal domains; user-N are people's accounts (root and system accounts are real); 198.18.x.x are private/internal IPs, 240.x.x.x public IPs (2001:db8:a:: and 2001:db8:b:: the same for IPv6); domain-N.tld are external domains (well-known vendors' domains are real); secret-N are secrets; numbers starting with 9 in URL paths and ?query-N are IDs and query strings; 02:00:00:... are MAC addresses. Use them exactly as written; treat them like the real values they stand for.
